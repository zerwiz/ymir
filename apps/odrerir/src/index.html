The Live Hall

the fleet's planning glass, carved into the cloth — read live, dealt by hand, never a write door

The hall keeps its own board, and it is the same one the fleet's mornings are read from: a tally of what stands, one call dealt at a time off a slate pile, and a carved ledger of what is underway, what has landed, and what is charted next. Every part of that board is here — rebuilt in the stone the rest of this page is cut from, with its own machinery intact.

What it deals are the hall's own errands — the work the fleet has armed, the smith who holds each one, and the carved ledger of what is underway, what has landed and what is charted next. Nothing here is a door: a word queued on this page stays in this page alone, and the hall's own board is where an errand is armed or released. If no snapshot is at hand the saga's own count stands in its place, and says so.
