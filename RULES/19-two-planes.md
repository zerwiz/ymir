# Rule 19 — Two planes: ours and the team's

> Adopted 2026-10-12, on the Allfather's word: *"my well are not the team well etc. we need
> to have clear distinctions on what's team functions and our functions."*

Rule 18 says which **class** a service belongs to. Rule 19 says whose **work** it serves.
These are different questions, and blurring them is how a teammate ends up reading the
operator's memory. So the heart runs **two planes**, and every function belongs to exactly
one of them.

## The law

```
plane_law[4]{rule}:
  "Two planes — OURS (the operator and his fleet) and THE TEAM'S (invited people, by login)"
  "A function belongs to ONE plane — never both, never blurred, never shared"
  "A team function is never a thin wrapper over one of our functions"
  "The answer assigns the gate (Rule 18): our functions sit behind OUR gates; the team's sit behind the team's"
```

## The test

> **If this function disappeared tomorrow, whose work would stop — mine, or the team's?**
> The answer assigns the plane.

## The team's — people with a login

```
theirs[6]: the places · the suite (Nextcloud Hub: Files · Talk · Calendar · Deck) ·
          the team's chat and meetings · the team's files and storage · the team's record ·
          the team's logins (SSO)
```

## Read each as absolute

1. **The well is ours.** There is **no team well.** A team that needs search over its own files
   gets a **team‑scoped index of a place** — a different thing, with a different owner, which
   never reads our memory. A teammate must not be able to ask the well anything.
2. **A teammate is never an agent's principal.** A teammate cannot command Brokk, read Runes,
   or see a plan. And our agents do not speak **as** a teammate: an agent acting in a place acts
   **as the operator**, and the place's record says so.
3. **There are two records.** *Our* record — Runes, the plans, the well — is never placed inside
   a team surface. *The place's* record — what was decided there — may be shown to its members.
4. **One host, two planes.** The server may run both, but that changes nothing: a service still
   belongs to one class (Rule 18) and one plane, and therefore to one gate.
