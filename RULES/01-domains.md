# Rule 01 — Domains, Houses, and Eindri

**Status:** law. This rule governs every other: how the work of the house is
divided, and who does it.

## 1. A house is a company — and only that

A **house** is a **company**. Nothing else is a house. **WayOf** is a house (a
company). Use "house" and "company" interchangeably; never use "house" for a
field of knowledge.

## 2. A domain is a field of knowledge

A **domain** is an area of knowledge, named for the figure whose craft it is.
There are eight:

| Domain | Id | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| **Ymir Labs** | `ymirlabs` | the system itself — agents knowledgeable about Ymir |
| **Brokk Forge** | `brokkforge` | engineering & build tooling |
| **Runestone Labs** | `runestone` | records & compliance |
| **Muninn Labs** | `muninn` | memory & knowledge |
| **Dvalin** | `dvalin` | crafted tools |
| **Utgard Studios** | `utgard` | creative |
| **Askr** | `askr` | human-centered products |
| **Mannheim** | `mannheim` | holding — allocated when a venture claims it |

A domain is **not** a company and **not** a workspace. Domains are the axes of
knowledge; workspaces (`personal` · `work`) are the scopes a person runs.

## 3. Eindri are the specialists

**Eindri** are the workers — a marketer, a builder, a researcher, a planner, a
reviewer, a documenter, a scout, and their kin. Every Eindri names **two**
things: its **domain** (which field it belongs to) and its **craft** (what it
does). An Eindri is never a domain and never a house.

- Agents knowledgeable about the **system itself** belong to **Ymir Labs**.
- A marketer belongs to a knowledge/market domain; a builder to the forge; a
  researcher to the well; and so on — by craft.

## 4. The counting rule

```
one_house   = many companies?  no — a house IS a company.
one_domain  = many Eindri.
one_eindri  = exactly one domain.
one_project = one workspace (company) + many domains.
```
