**Rules for filling this template**

1. The order is fixed. A reader who was not in the room must be able to act from
   this document alone.
2. Never paraphrase inside a quote. If the transcript is wrong, say so in the
   rationale, not in the quote.
3. A decision without a rationale says `not stated`. It does not invent one.
4. An action without a due date says `date unresolved` and carries a `decide_by`.
5. No name is assigned unless the transcript names the person.
