# Participant brief

Sent to one person, containing only what that person needs.

**Include**
- Their actions: deliverable, due date, definition of done, dependency.
- The decisions that affect their work, one line each.
- What they owe others, and what others owe them.
- The date by which they must reply with a correction.

**Do not include**
- The full minutes. Link to the single authoritative copy instead.
- Other people's action lists.
- Transcript excerpts longer than the evidence quote.

**Closing line**
> If anything here is wrong, reply with the correction by the end of the next
> business day. Silence is recorded as provisional acceptance.
