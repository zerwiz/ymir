# 0.1.123 — Eir learns the platform: a hand's lawful absences are SKIP, not broken

## What the measurement showed

On `sleipnir` (`role=hand`, Android), `bin/agents/eir-doctor.sh check` reported **ten** red
surfaces, and only some were faults:

```
vault      broken   cryptsetup cannot exist on Android; the vault is a server property
shells     broken   Electron is not built for bionic
sessrumnir broken   same
autoboot   broken   no systemd — there is no boot unit to enable
herdr      broken   absent — but tmux is installed, and it is the other pane backend
well       ok       ← a FALSE GREEN: nothing was listening on :4602 at all
```

Two real breaks (`snotra`, `voice`) hid among six false ones, and one green row was a lie: the
`well` surface answered `ok` from the **presence of two scripts**, never from the well itself.

> A health report that fails the impossible teaches the reader to ignore it. Rule 25: probe,
> never assume — and a clean skip is a pass.

## The fix

- The doctor resolves `PLATFORM` (Termux when `$PREFIX/etc` exists) and `ROLE` (from
  `.ymir-seat`). On a `hand` the surfaces that cannot exist here are reported **`skip`** with the
  reason — `vault`, `shells`, `sessrumnir`, `autoboot` — and skips never count as broken.
- `herdr` is satisfied by `tmux` (`surface_ok`), and its detail names the fallback version.
- `s_well` now **asks the port** (`/dev/tcp/127.0.0.1/4602`). A silent door is red, and the
  detail says why: *"door silent on :4602 (role hand: the well is the heart door)"*.
- The mend loop skips skipped surfaces, so `eir fix` no longer tries to install cryptsetup on a
  phone.

## Verified

```
before: eir[1]{action,root,broken}: "check",…,10
after : "check",…,6        ← snotra, voice, well, arm, hoard, ward — the real ones
        sessrumnir/shells/autoboot/vault → skip, each with its reason
        herdr → ok, "tmux fallback: tmux 3.6b"
        well  → broken, "door silent on :4602 (role hand: the well is the heart door)"
```
