## hoard · 0.2.0 · 2026-10-24 — a company we serve had nowhere to be: place client_of, project client, and the register's own validator

### Why
Plan 128, phase 1. hodd/identity held companies.yaml (the houses we ARE), collaborators.yaml (people) and projects.yaml (repos) — and no way to say that a company is one we SERVE. The one client-shaped realm the home already carried, inmostinvest, was filed as a house with its owner among its members, the relationship living only in prose. The register hodd/identity/clients.yaml was written by hand; this change gives it its support in the repo. bin/identity/clients.sh validate|check|load FAILS LOUDLY on a bad shape (an invented state, a served_by that names no house, an owner who is not a person, a realm or place that does not exist) — and its check() no longer refuses every client on a healthy home: it joined a realm onto the hodd root (home/hodd/svartalfaheim/opticat) and a place onto a doubled hodd/identity path, so a correct register read as broken. bin/identity/place-validate.sh now decides the place relation the plan states: a place whose company is a CLIENT must declare client_of: <house-id> naming a house in companies.yaml, and without it the place is REFUSED in a sentence naming the missing field; a place that declares client_of beside no register is refused naming the file and the fix; a company that is neither house nor client is reported, never invented. The company is read from the field or from places/<company>/[<place>]/place.yaml, so a place written without a company: field still resolves. --all walks the whole shelf at both depths and exits non-zero when any place is refused. bin/identity/projects-validate.sh resolves the optional client: field against the register. Proven by place-validate.sh --self-test (7 checks) and .agents/tests/clients-register.test.sh. inmostinvest is NOT re-filed: that is the Allfather's word, not a migration's.
