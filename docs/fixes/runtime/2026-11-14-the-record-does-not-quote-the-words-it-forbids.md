# The record does not quote the words it forbids

**Plan:** 275 (repair of the agnostic repair). **Date:** 2026-11-14.

## What was wrong

The note that recorded *"no machine of ours in an added line"* printed the offending lines to
show what had been wrong. So the record of the law carried the very names the law forbids — the
check that decides whether the branch is agnostic reads every added line, and a quotation is an
added line.

A record is not exempt from the rule it records.

## The fix

The note now describes the fault in **roles** — the two machines the testing law named — and
keeps every measurement (33 lines / 3,569 bytes at a start, 34 / 3,745 at a compaction, a
budget of 60 lines and 8 KiB). Only the house figure in the lore remains, because a figure is
not a machine.

## The lesson worth keeping

When a law forbids a word, the note that enforces it says *"the machine name"*, never the name.
The reader does not need the string to understand the fault; the checker does need it absent.
