## runtime · 1.2.1 · 2026-10-10 — the MCP gateway could not find its engine or its journal scripts (the bin move missed its callers)

### What

Two wounds, same cause: a `bin/` move left two callers behind, and the gateway
degraded silently.

1. **The engine was unreachable on a fresh start.** `bin/bridge/mcp-gateway.sh`
   computed `ROOT="$SCRIPT_DIR/.."` — which, after the move to `bin/bridge/`,
   resolves to `bin/`, not the repo root. So `server_path()` looked for
   `bin/tools/mcp-gateway/server.mjs`, found nothing, and `serve` died
   `error: mcp-gateway engine not found`. The live process had predated the move,
   so nothing surfaced it. While this branch was open, main healed this half by a
   better road — the Rule-12 walk up to the tree that owns `.pi/` and `RULES/`,
   which does not count `..` at all. This change keeps main's resolver and drops
   the branch's earlier `$SCRIPT_DIR/../..`; only the journal half below is its
   surviving work.

2. **`skuld_sync`'s reconcile silently no-opped.** `tools/mcp-gateway/server.mjs`
   spawned `path.join(BIN, "journal-reconcile.sh")` with `BIN=bin/bridge`, but the
   journal scripts live in `bin/records/`. The sync printed `bash: …/bin/bridge/
   journal-reconcile.sh: No such file or directory` and the write-queue was never
   pushed. Added a `journalScript(name)` resolver (tries `BIN/`, `BIN/records/`,
   `BIN/../records/`) used by both `journalCall` and `journalReconcile`, so a
   future move cannot repeat it.

Also mapped the gateway paths to the `runtime` component in the fixes guard
(`tools/mcp-gateway/*`, `bin/bridge/mcp-gateway.sh`, `bin/records/journal-*.sh`),
which the guard had not learned after the move.

### Verified

- `.agents/tests/mcp-gateway.test.sh` — **ALL PASS**. Before the fix it failed
  three assertions (`journal empty`, `journal entry shape`, `sync push`), and in a
  worktree the engine could not be found at all. After: a detached write lands in
  the journal, the sync flushes it to the heart inbox, and a reconnect proxies live.
- `node --check tools/mcp-gateway/server.mjs` and `bash -n bin/bridge/mcp-gateway.sh`
  clean.
