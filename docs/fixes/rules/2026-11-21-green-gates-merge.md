# rules · 2026-11-21 — RULES/08: if it passes the gates, it merges

## What changed

At the Allfather's word (*"If it pass the gates. We merge. We need to update the rules."*), RULES/08 gains
an appended section. A PR whose required gates are green, which has no conflict and no hold, is merged by the
heart's keeper, by an arm holder for its own PR, or by the operator from his hall. The merge is made under the
operator's own account, with one Runes entry per merge. A red, pending or conflicted PR is never merged
automatically; the operator may still merge it from his hall with a second, explicit click.

## Why

PRs waited for hours for a seal when the gates had already judged them, and `main` moved under them meanwhile.
The gates are the seal.
