**Component:** snotra

## What changed

Added three new Snotra automation scripts implementing Plan 143 phases 3 and 4:

### Phase 3 — Meeting rhythm automation
- `snotra-friday-synch.sh/py`: Friday Synch trigger that posts status updates
  on This Week cards after a Friday meeting. Comments on each card with the
  owner's progress.
- `snotra-grooming.sh/py`: Monthly Grooming card on "Next Meeting Agenda"
  that triages the Backlog into overdue, due-soon, and backlog items.

### Phase 4 — Card-movement rules
- `snotra-card-moves.sh/py`: watcher implementing Rule A (card moved to
  This Week with a member → assign + notify + suggested steps checklist),
  Rule B (card moved to Done with sign-off needed → move to To Sign-off;
  mis-move → move back + note), and note-on-mis-move for every automated
  correction.

### Wiring
- Updated `snotra-cron.sh` with `friday-synch`, `grooming`, and `card-moves`
  commands.
- Added cron entries to `config/cron.yaml.example`.
- Updated Example8 — Meetings place profile to the 8-list template.
