# snotra · 2026-11-13 — the collaborator-onboarding shape is a file, not a row in an index

## Files
- `systems/snotra/services/letters/templates/house/collaborator-onboarding.md` (new)

## Why
The house letters shelf declares four shapes in its index and held three: `collaborator-onboarding.md` was a row in
`templates/house/README.md` and no file. So the one shape that fits *giving a person access* — what they get, what they
are, what they are not, the way back in, the one first step — had to be invented at the desk each time, and an invented
order is how a letter ends up with two asks and a credential nobody can reset.

Measured on a real send (2026-11-13, a client's suite login re-enabled): the letter was written in the desk's own words
and dressed by the default rendering template, because the shape that fits was not on the shelf to fill.

## What changed
- The shape exists, in the same form as its three neighbours: a header, a subject line, the ordered blocks with
  `<placeholders>`, `## After sending`, `## Rules this shape holds`, `## CHANGE LOG`.
- The order it fixes, and why each block is where it is: **what you get** (one login, the doors in the order they will
  meet them) · **CHECKPOINT** (a first sign-in described in words they can check) · **what you are** (the place and the
  rights that match) · **what you are not** (the closed surfaces, named, closing with "say the word and it opens") ·
  **if the key fails** (the reset path, so the credential has a way back) · **the one first step** (one bounded act on
  real work, never "have a look around").
- Five rules the shape carries: one credential per letter · the letter is the only place the person reads the key, the
  vault the only place we keep it · the first step is on real work · one address, the door they sign in at, never a path
  into the home · what is not open is written down.

## Proof
The index row and the file now agree — `ls templates/house/` lists `collaborator-onboarding.md` beside `follow-up.md`,
`lesson-share.md` and `setup-walkthrough.md`. The shape was filled against the 2026-11-13 send as a draft (drafted, read
back, discarded; no second letter to the same person) and the five blocks each mapped to a sentence a reader can act on.
