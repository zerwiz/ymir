## skills · unversioned · 2026-10-20 — grades and open questions

### What

- Decisions carry a **grade**: A (one-way / strategic), B (costly to reverse), C (easily
  reversed), with the grading rule printed in the log.
- The renderer writes `questions.md`: every open question, its decider, the date it is
  needed by, whether it blocks, and its evidence.
- The schema accepts `grade` on decisions and `status` on questions.

### Why

The pilot recorded six decisions; the transcript carries more, including the agreement,
the code foundation, the shortest-path pilot and the provisional patent. Six was the
extractor's threshold, not the meeting's count. Open questions were being buried in the
follow-up instead of held as their own register.

  11 documents rendered.
