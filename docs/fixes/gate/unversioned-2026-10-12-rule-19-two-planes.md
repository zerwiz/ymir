## gate · unversioned · 2026-10-12 — Rule 19: two planes (ours and the team's)

### What

The Allfather: *"my well are not the team well etc. we need to have clear distinctions on
what's team functions and our functions."* Rule 18 says which **class** a service is; Rule 19
says whose **work** it serves — a different question, and blurring them is how a teammate
ends up reading the operator's memory.

**Two planes, and a function belongs to exactly one:**

- **OURS** — the well (Mimirsbrunn) · Runes · the plan ledger + Skuld · Hnitbjörg and the
  confidential session · Brokk, Eindri and the agents · the A2A mesh · Hlidskjalf/Óðrerir ·
  the hoard.
- **THE TEAM'S** — the places · the suite (Nextcloud Hub) · their chat and meetings · their
  files and storage · their record · their logins.

The three sharpest separations: **there is no team well** (a team's search is a place-scoped
index that never reads our memory) · **a teammate is never an agent's principal** (and an
agent acting in a place acts as the operator, and the record says so) · **two records** (ours
is never placed in a team surface; the place's record may be shown to its members).

The test: *if this function disappeared tomorrow, whose work would stop — mine, or the team's?*
