## gate · unversioned · 2026-10-13 — Rule 20: company documents are written in business language

### What

A document that leaves the company — to a client, partner, teammate, supplier or regulator — is
read by people who do not work here and do not know our internal names. Rule 20 makes the
language of such a document **plain professional English**, and keeps internal shorthand out of
it:

- **no internal names** — not a system, a component, an agent, a hall, a rune, or a rule number;
- **no persona and no allegory** — the writer is the company; describe a thing by what it does;
- **no infrastructure detail** — no hostnames, ports, container names, paths, IPs or account ids;
- **no third party the reader need not know** — another product, project or company is "another
  product", or is named only when it is genuinely part of the subject;
- a document that cannot be written without internal terms is an **internal** document — marked,
  and kept in.

It carries a seven-question **send check** and a table of preferred plain forms.
