**Ten shelves out of fifteen cannot be navigated.** This is the concrete mechanism behind *"we
don't know where the documents are"* and *"features land in the wrong place"* — a shelf with no
index is invisible to a reader who is not already holding the path.

**Why it reports rather than fails, today:** turning it strict would red the build on **10 real
shelves in the operator's own home**. A gate that fires before the work is done gets ignored, and
an ignored gate is the disease this whole plan is against. So it is wired into `ci-verify` as a
**report** with the count visible, and `--strict` is one flag away — to be flipped when the shelves
are indexed, not before.

The staleness heuristic is explicit about being a heuristic: it counts names in the index against
the files on the shelf and calls a shelf stale below half. A wrong index is worse than a missing
one, which is why both are reported.
