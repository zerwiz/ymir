
### Also in this change — the front door says what the chain is

`README.md` gained the long form of **The Allfather's Chain**: the chain is the right to say
*go* and *stop*, drawn in the record; the gates are that hand mechanised — a gate may only
refuse, never permit, or it would be a second master; and the chain breaks by small mercies
(a self-merged PR, a token pasted into a chat, a gate skipped "just this once"), never by one
dramatic act. The first draft of that section said *"no gate between the agent and the tools"*,
which contradicts every ward this repository ships; the rewritten line keeps the Allfather's
meaning — nothing else commands — without unmaking the gates that keep his caution while he
sleeps.
