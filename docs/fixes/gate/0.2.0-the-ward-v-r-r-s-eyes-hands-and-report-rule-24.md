## gate · 0.2.0 · 2026-10-18 — The ward — Vörðr's eyes, hands and report (Rule 24)

### Why
The house had 16 laws for its agents and none for its doors: measured 2026-10-18, a public tunnel answered 200 with no login, ssh sat on every interface with password auth at its default, the control plane was allowed from any address, the vault key was world-readable, and nothing watched the host. bin/ward/security-audit.sh measures (ports, ufw by source, effective sshd policy, tunnels and their auth, secret modes, watchers) and exits 1 on HIGH/CRITICAL; bin/ward/verdr.sh walks, writes hodd/memory/ward/<date>.md and speaks on HIGH; bin/ward/harden.sh prints every mend with its revert line, applies --sshd (backup, sshd -t validation, rollback) and --ssh-keys-perm, and REFUSES --ufw without an explicit flag because the box travels. Eir gains a ward surface. RULES/24-the-ward.md is the law. Tests .agents/tests/ward.test.sh 16/16, fixture-driven so a clean box and an open box are both provable.
