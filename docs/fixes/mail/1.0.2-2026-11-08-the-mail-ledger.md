## mail · 1.0.2 · 2026-11-08 — the mail ledger: what went out, what came in, and the shapes we send

### What

The house could say what it had **sent** (an approval record) and what it had **read** (Gná's store), but not, in one
place, when a letter left and to whom. An approval is an ACT, not a letter; a store row is an ARRIVAL, not a
conversation. Asked plainly: *"do we have a system over send emails and incoming emails"* — outbound yes, inbound yes, and
**no single record of either**.

- **`bin/mail/ledger.sh`** — one door over both directions: `out` (append), `in` (read the store), `tail` (both, newest
  first), `digest` (per-day counts), `self-test`.
- **The record is the home's**, at the mail shelf beside the store, and it is **append-only**: a row is never edited, a
  correction is a new row. Who we write to and what about is private; it never goes in the repo.
- **`mail-send.sh send` now records the letter as it leaves** (version 1.0.2). The headers are read back **from the draft
  that was actually sent**, so the ledger says what left rather than what we believe we asked for. A ledger failure warns;
  it never fails a letter that is already gone.
- **One message, one row.** The store and the ledger both name the same letter — the store when the importer reads the sent
  folder, the ledger at the moment of sending — so `tail` and `digest` dedupe on the message id (and, where there is none,
  on direction + moment + subject). Without that, every outbound letter would count twice a day later.
- **`templates/house/`** — the *shapes* of the letters we send, in markdown, beside the vendored HTML templates that render
  them: collaborator onboarding, lesson share, setup walkthrough, follow-up. A shape is the order that makes a letter work,
  not boilerplate.

### Measured, before and after

- The store held **246 messages**, and it already carried both directions (`direction: in|out`) — the outbound rows arrived
  only when the importer next read the sent folder, which is exactly the lag that hid two letters sent today until they
  were backfilled.
- After: `bin/mail/ledger.sh tail 5` shows both directions in one list, and `digest --days 2` prints `2026-11-08 → out 2,
  in 1`.
- `bin/mail/ledger.sh self-test` — append-only, and a quote in a subject cannot break a row.
