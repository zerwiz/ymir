## docs · 2026-10-21 — the front door names Skírnir

### Why
The README named the crew and then said almost nothing about the one figure who speaks to people
the Allfather has never met. Skírnir was one clause in a list. His walls — the four refusals that
make him safe to point at strangers — were documented under `docs/features/` and invisible on the
front door, where a reader decides whether to trust the project at all.

### Fix
A section, **Skírnir — the mouth with no hands**: who he is (Freyr's messenger, in his lord's
place, sword left behind), the four walls in his own terms — an approved digest that never reads
the vault, a quarantine with no tools or credentials, a gate that fails CLOSED on a canary, and a
ledger of every question and refusal — and the line that makes the design legible: *a mouth with
no hands is not a limitation to fix later; an assistant who can only speak cannot open a door for
someone who is not the Allfather.*
