## docs · 2026-10-21 — the front door names the crew, the skills, and the engines

### Why
The Allfather read the README and said what a stranger would say: *"what about the agents and
skills? we have missed a lot… and we missed the hound."* He was right on every count. The front
door named Brokk, Skírnir, Snotra and the council, and then left **twenty-two figures**, the whole
skill shelf, and every engine under a Norse name invisible. A reader could not learn that Kvasir
scouts or that Garmr is the hound, because nothing said so.

The table I first wrote for "the rest of the hall" made it worse, and he named that too: a list of
descriptors is not an explanation. It is replaced with prose sections.

### Fix
Three new sections, all prose:

- **The crew — twenty-two figures and one assistant**, grouped by where a figure works (the hall, the
  forge, the field, around the house), each with the job its name promises, and Skírnir set apart
  because he is not on the bench.
- **The skills — craft on demand, never preloaded**: why nothing is preloaded, the four shelves and
  the order they are searched, why a shipped skill beats a local one on purpose, and the rule that a
  synthesised skill is not trusted until it is validated and indexed.
- **The house itself — the engines under the names**: Yggdrasil, Utgard, Mimirsbrunn, Runes, Sága,
  Nornir, Skuld, Mjollnir, Ratatoskr, Bifröst, Heimdall, Hlidskjalf, Herdr, Hófvarpnir, Vörðr — and
  **Garmr the hound**, named with the distinction that matters: a ward refuses, the hound watches.

And "the rest of the hall" was rewritten from a twelve-row table into eight prose sections with the
figures' real names (Gladsheim, Brimir, Hófvarpnir, Hnitbjörg, Vörðr, Garmr, the Þing, Sól,
Mánagandr, Sessrúmnir, Þráðr), each explaining the thing rather than labelling it.

One honest correction recorded inside: the naming map still files **Gladsheim** as planned while the
machine answers `gladsheim.sh status` with a live room. The map is behind the machine, and the README
now says so instead of repeating the map.
