| Collaboration | **Hand a brief to a worker — `eindri-send`** (`bin/agents/eindri-send.sh` · [how-to](docs/features/hand-a-brief-to-a-worker.md)) | Plan 293 part 5. One door to put a line in front of a seated worker here or on another machine, and the three things a runbook paragraph could not enforce: it **reads the input box first and refuses when it holds text** (never clears it — those words may be the Allfather's), it **stamps every line** with who sent it, from which pane and which machine, and after sending it **waits then asks three witnesses** — `herdr agent get`, the screen, and the box — pressing Enter when the words stand typed but unsent, sending once more only when the line was lost, and doing neither when it cannot tell. A far machine is probed, never assumed: `agent prompt` where herdr has it, `agent send` + `pane send-keys … Enter` on 0.7.x, and the machine's road comes from the home's fleet registry. Reads the box with `bin/seat/herdr-editor.py`.
