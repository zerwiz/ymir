| Voice | **Check the microphone** (`bin/voice/mic-check.sh`, Rödd's Settings) | Shows what the machine hears: the level each second in dBFS, the peak, the spread and the band (silent · too quiet · good · clipping), with the system's input volume. Rödd's Settings carries the same line live, plus a ten-second check that says whether a voice stayed in one band. Nothing is recorded during a call and no audio is kept | [docs/features/check-the-microphone.md](docs/features/check-the-microphone.md) |
