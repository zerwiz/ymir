| Security | **Garmr — the switch is one word: `off` and `on`** | The hound could be paused, but only for a moment: `pause` stopped the two units and wrote its marker, and then a reboot, a re-install, or Gróa's `refresh` raised him again — a guardian you put down came back without asking. Now there is **one word each way**, and the word **stands**: `sudo bin/ward/garmr.sh off` stops the hound and the judgement, writes root's marker **and** a readable copy the tower and housewatch can see, and **disables** the units, so nothing raises him behind your word; `on` clears it, enables and starts. `status` answers with a `mode` line — `OFF (since …)`, never "all clear" — and reads without root; `off --dry-run` prints the plan without root at all. `pause`/`resume` remain as the same switch under its old names. Root stays required on purpose (a guardian an ordinary process can silence is the guardian the intruder silences first) and the smoke test now asserts a non-root `off` **and** `on` are both refused. These rows sat under "planned, not yet built" since 2026-10-21, though the hound has been running that whole time; this one is filed where it belongs | [docs/features/the-guardian.md](docs/features/the-guardian.md) |
