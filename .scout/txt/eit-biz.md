# EIT business creation support

Ready to turn your idea into impact? EIT Business Creation programmes connect you with the resources, networks and support to grow. Whether at the idea stage, a start-up or scaling up, we offer the essential ingredients to bring your venture to life through:

- financing opportunities;
- access to market;
- entrepreneurial training and mentorship;
- marketing and PR support;
- matchmaking with partners and investors through the EIT’s Europe‑wide network.

You’ll find these ingredients no matter which of our Knowledge and Innovation Communities (KICs) you choose to join. Each one will connect you to a Europe‑wide network of like‑minded businesses and innovators, all committed to helping you achieve your goals,

If your business is working to make Europe and the world more sustainable, our KICs offer the incubation, acceleration and support services you need.

Our programmes have already helped launch standout ventures across sectors. Here are a few examples:

- [Alchemie Technology](https://www.alchemietechnology.com/) , a Climate-KIC Accelerator alumnus, is leading the sustainable textile industry with its low-carbon, digital dyeing innovations.

- [Waybler](https://waybler.com/en/home) , backed by EIT Urban Mobility, is scaling efficient EV charging solutions to support Europe’s transition to electric transport.

### Empowering inclusion in tech and business

Our core mission at the European Institute of Innovation and Technology (EIT) is to fight unequal access to opportunities in business and tech, especially for historically excluded groups such as women.

Women’s participation in the world of innovation is critical both to successfully steering tech towards sustainability and to avoiding the reproduction of existing inequalities. That’s why we created programmes specifically designed to give women the skills and support they need to break into the tech world.
