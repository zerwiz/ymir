Multiannual Work Programme

The Chips Joint Undertaking (Chips JU) Multiannual Work Programme covers the activities of the Chips JU from 2023 to 2027, both for the Electronic Components and Systems Research and Innovation (ECS R&I), and the Chips for Europe Initiative parts, including call descriptions, budgets, call conditions and activities in support to operations.

The links below lead you to the latest versions of the Multiannual Work Programme documents. Earlier versions can be found in the tab “General Documents”.
