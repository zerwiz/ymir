Questions

The AI on Demand Platform (AIoD) is Europe’s trusted gateway for discovering, deploying and scaling AI. It connects AI solutions, providers, expertise, infrastructure and ecosystem actors in one trusted European environment. Whether you are a startup, SME, enterprise, public organisation, researcher or innovation partner, AIoD helps you find AI solutions, connect with trusted providers and partners, access tools and resources, and explore opportunities for testing, deployment and scaling across Europe.

The AIoD Marketplace and the Business Navigator are complementary services within the AI on Demand Platform. The Marketplace helps users discover AI products, services, tools and infrastructure from European providers. The Business Navigator helps users explore Europe’s AI ecosystem by identifying relevant organisations, partners, opportunities and support actors. Together, they make it easier to find both AI offerings and the organisations behind them.

The Business Navigator focuses on ecosystem discovery, visibility and matchmaking. It helps users identify relevant AI providers, partners and opportunities across Europe. The Marketplace focuses on presenting AI products and services, enabling users to explore concrete AI offerings and connect with potential providers. In short, the Business Navigator helps you find the right organisations, while the Marketplace helps you find the right AI solutions.

Yes. AI companies can already register in the Business Navigator to increase their visibility across the European AI ecosystem and present their offerings. Currently, Marketplace offerings are provided by partners of the DeployAI consortium developing the platform. Access for a broader range of European organisations and AI providers will be expanded progressively through future platform releases.

AIoD helps organisations develop, assess and deploy AI in a more trustworthy and responsible way. The platform provides tools, resources and workflows that support risk classification, self-assessment, testing, documentation and evidence generation. These capabilities help organisations evaluate legal, ethical and operational aspects of AI systems, improve transparency, and prepare for AI Act compliance requirements. While AIoD can support AI Act readiness and responsible deployment, it does not replace legal advice or formal compliance assessments.

AIoD connects and strengthens existing European AI initiatives rather than replacing them. It brings together research, industry, public-sector actors, innovation hubs and infrastructure providers to help turn AI capabilities into operational impact, supported by tools such as an AI marketplace, low-code development options and secure solutions for generative AI and large language models. 

The AIoD research section provides access to European AI projects, research results and expertise, making it easier to discover innovative technologies with real-world application potential. By bringing together European-funded AI initiatives in a shared ecosystem, it increases the visibility and accessibility of research outcomes, helps organisations identify relevant innovations, and supports the transfer of research into industry by connecting AI innovations with organisations seeking practical deployment opportunities.
