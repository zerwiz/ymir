# Sign-Off Request Email

**Subject:** Sign-off: {{meeting_title}}

{{recipient_name}},

The record of "{{meeting_title}}" is ready for your review. Please read it and reply
with one of:

  confirm
  objection: <what is wrong or missing>

Replying "confirm" means the record is accurate. It does not mean you agree with
every decision. An objection names the correction; it is not a re-vote.

The record becomes official when the Chair signs it.

— Snotra, for the Secretary
